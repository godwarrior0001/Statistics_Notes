# Day 4: Probability & Distributions

## 1. Probability Basics & Bayes' Theorem
* **Probability**: $P(A) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span).
* **Bayes' Theorem**: $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$ forming the foundation of Naive Bayes classifiers[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span).

## 2. Key Distributions
* **Binomial Distribution**: Models $n$ independent trials with success probability $p$[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span).
* **Poisson Distribution**: Models counts of rare events occurring at average rate $\lambda$[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span).
* **Normal (Gaussian) Distribution**: Symmetric, bell-shaped continuous distribution described by $\mu$ and $\sigma$[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
* **Empirical (68-95-99.7) Rule**: ~68% of data lie within $\pm 1\sigma$, ~95% within $\pm 2\sigma$, and ~99.7% within $\pm 3\sigma$[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span).

## 3. Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
* For sufficiently large samples ($n \ge 30$), the sampling distribution of the sample mean is approximately normal regardless of population shape[span_50](start_span)[span_50](end_span).
