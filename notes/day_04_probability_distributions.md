# Day 4: Probability & Distributions

## 1. Probability Basics & Bayes' Theorem
* **Probability**: P(A) = favourable outcomes / total outcomes[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span).
* **Bayes' Theorem**: P(A|B) = [P(B|A) * P(A)] / P(B), forming the foundation of Naive Bayes classifiers[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).

## 2. Key Distributions
* **Binomial Distribution**: Models $n$ independent trials with success probability $p$[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).
* **Poisson Distribution**: Models counts of rare events occurring at an average rate $\lambda$[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).
* **Normal (Gaussian) Distribution**: Symmetric, bell-shaped continuous distribution described by mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span).
* **Empirical (68-95-99.7) Rule**: ~68% of data lie within $\pm 1\sigma$, ~95% within $\pm 2\sigma$, and ~99.7% within $\pm 3\sigma$ of the mean[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span).

## 3. Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
* For sufficiently large samples ($n \ge 30$), the sampling distribution of the sample mean is approximately normal regardless of the population shape[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span).
