# Day 1: Foundations of Statistics & Data Types

## 1. What is Statistics?
* **Definition**: Statistics is the science of collecting, organising, summarising, analysing, interpreting, and presenting data to support decision-making under uncertainty[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). It acts as the grammar of data science[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
* **Branches**:
  * **Descriptive Statistics**: Summarises and describes the data actually held (e.g., mean, median, standard deviation)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
  * **Inferential Statistics**: Uses a sample to draw probabilistic conclusions about a larger population[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

## 2. Population vs. Sample & Parameters vs. Statistics
* **Population (N)**: The entire collection of units of interest[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
* **Sample (n)**: A subset actually observed[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Parameter**: A fixed numerical fact about the population (Greek letters: mu, sigma, rho)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* **Statistic**: A number computed from a sample to estimate a parameter (Roman letters: x-bar, s, r)[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

## 3. Scales of Measurement (Stevens)
* **Nominal**: Labels with no order (City, Department, Gender)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **Ordinal**: Ordered categories with no fixed gap (Grades: A > B > C)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* **Interval**: Ordered with equal gaps, but arbitrary zero (Temperature in Celsius)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
* **Ratio**: Ordered with equal gaps and a true zero (Salary, Marks, Age)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

## AI / Data Science Connection
* Data types dictate preprocessing steps: Nominal variables require **One-Hot Encoding**, Ordinal variables require **Ordinal Encoding**, and continuous variables require **Scaling/Normalisation**[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
