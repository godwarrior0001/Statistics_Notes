# Day 2: Measures of Central Tendency

## 1. Arithmetic Mean & Weighted Mean
* **Arithmetic Mean**: The sum of all observations divided by their count: $\overline{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i$[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).
* **Weighted Mean**: Used when observations carry different importance weights $w_i$: $\overline{x}_w = \frac{\sum w_i x_i}{\sum w_i}$[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span).

## 2. Median & Mode
* **Median**: The middle value when data are sorted (Position = $\frac{n+1}{2}$). It is robust and unaffected by extreme outliers[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).
* **Mode**: The most frequently occurring value; the only measure usable for nominal data[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).

## AI / Data Science Connection
* Central tendency powers missing-value imputation[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span): fill normal numeric features with the mean, skewed features (like income) with the median, and categorical features with the mode[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span).
