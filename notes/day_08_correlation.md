# Day 8: Correlation

## 1. Metrics
* **Covariance**: Measures joint directional movement, but its magnitude depends heavily on data units[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span).
* **Pearson Correlation Coefficient ($r$)**: Standardises covariance into the range $[-1, +1]$ to measure linear association strength[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span).
* **Spearman's Rank ($\rho$)**: Non-parametric rank correlation robust to outliers and monotonic non-linear links[span_55](start_span)[span_55](end_span).
